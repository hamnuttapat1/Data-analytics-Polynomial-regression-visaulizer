What counts as convergence?

Play stops if MSE ≤ 10⁻¹⁰, the gradient norm ≤ 10⁻⁵, or the MSE change is at most 10⁻⁶ × max(1, previous MSE) for 20 consecutive epochs. The last rule is a practical plateau check, not a guarantee of an exact optimum. Reaching the maximum epoch limit is reported separately.

A higher degree can fit more complex curves, but a lower training loss does not guarantee better predictions on new data.
